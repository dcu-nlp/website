Cilter is a child-protection technology project focused on detecting and blocking cyberbullying, self-harm, and grooming behaviours in messaging apps and browsers. The project aims to deliver a market-ready smartphone solution that supports parents and caregivers through earlier detection of harmful online content.

Public DCU project information lists James O'Higgins Norman and Brian Davis as principal investigators and identifies Joachim Wagner as part of the research team. The project brings together NLP, computer vision, and online-safety research in a clearly applied setting.
