BullyBench is a public benchmark that treats cyberbullying as a socially complex, multi-role problem rather than a narrow binary classification task.

It was developed with adolescents through a youth-in-the-loop process and paired with experts-in-the-loop evaluation for realism, relevance, and coherence. The result is a public resource that connects NLP evaluation with participation, ethics, and online safety.
