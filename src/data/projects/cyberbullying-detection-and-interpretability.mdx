This project area brings together public papers on cross-platform cyberbullying detection, interpretability for transformer models, and the limitations of treating online harms as a simple binary classification problem.

Taken together, these publications show the group's progression from benchmarking classifiers, to examining interpretability, to rethinking the problem formulation itself in more realistic intervention contexts.
