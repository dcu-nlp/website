Brian Davis is an Associate Professor in the School of Computing at Dublin City University. He also serves as Faculty Associate Dean for Teaching and Learning in DCU's Faculty of Engineering and Computing.

His work brings together computational linguistics research and practical language engineering. At DCU, he has built a research group working on structured language generation, recruitment-text bias detection, and cyberbullying detection in noisy short-text settings.

Before joining DCU in 2019, he was a Lecturer at Maynooth University. From 2014 to 2017, he was a Research Fellow, Adjunct Lecturer, and Research Unit Leader at the INSIGHT Centre for Data Analytics at NUI Galway, where he led the Knowledge Discovery Unit.

Public DCU and Anti-Bullying Centre material also highlights his work on online safety and child-protection technologies. He is listed as a principal investigator on the Cilter project and as the lead of DCU's NLP applications and Irish language technology group.

Earlier in his career, he coordinated the Horizon 2020 SSIX project and led SFI co-funded targeted projects with Elsevier and DataLive, reflecting a long-running interest in collaborative research with clear deployment goals.
