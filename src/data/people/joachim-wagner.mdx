Joachim Wagner is a Research Fellow in the School of Computing at Dublin City University. His research spans text classification, neural network interpretability, multilingual parsing, and user-generated content.

He has a background in applied machine learning and high-performance computing, with a Magister Artium in computational linguistics and artificial intelligence from Universitat Osnabruck and a PhD in Computing from DCU.

Recent public work links him to Irish-language NLP and cyberbullying research, including collaborative publications on BullyBench, synthetic data for cyberbullying detection, and work on the complexity of online harms.
