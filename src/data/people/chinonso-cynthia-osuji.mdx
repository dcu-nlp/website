Chinonso Cynthia Osuji is a PhD researcher in the School of Computing at Dublin City University.

Her work focuses on natural language processing, especially pipeline approaches to data-to-text generation.

Her recent public work includes INLG 2024 papers on pipeline neural data-to-text generation with large language models and the group's contribution to the GEM 2024 multilingual data-to-text generation task.
