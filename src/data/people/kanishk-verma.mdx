Kanishk Verma is an Irish Research Council and Google-funded PhD researcher whose work focuses on detecting and understanding cyberbullying among children and teenagers.

His work spans cyberbullying detection, interpretability, youth-informed dataset creation, and safer online systems. Public outputs include papers on cross-platform cyberbullying benchmarks, transformer interpretability, position papers on moving beyond binary framings of online harms, and the BullyBench dataset.

His work has been recognised through an Early Stage Researcher of the Year award in December 2024 and an Honourable Mention at ACM IDC 2025 for research on youth participation in understanding cyberbullying.
