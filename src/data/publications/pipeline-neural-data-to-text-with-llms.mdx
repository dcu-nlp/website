This paper extends prior work on pipeline neural language generation by integrating pretrained language models into both pipeline and end-to-end data-to-text systems. It reports that fine-tuned PLMs perform strongly on automatic metrics, while a streamlined three-stage pipeline produces especially strong human-evaluated fluency and adequacy.

The work reflects the group's interest in practical, controllable data-to-text generation.
