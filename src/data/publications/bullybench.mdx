This paper introduces BullyBench, a cyberbullying benchmark built with young people and evaluated with experts-in-the-loop. It grounds dataset quality in realism, relevance, and coherence rather than treating cyberbullying as a simple binary labeling problem.

It also reports baseline results for encoder-only and decoder-only language models on multi-class cyberbullying role classification, creating a stronger public starting point for future online-safety NLP research.
