This paper examines whether synthetic data and LLM-generated labels can help address the shortage of ethically collectable, child-relevant cyberbullying data. The results show that BERT-based classifiers trained with synthetic or LLM-labeled data can approach the performance of models trained with fully authentic labeled data.

For the group, the paper highlights an applied research theme that combines language modeling with online safety constraints, annotation ethics, and practical moderation workflows.
