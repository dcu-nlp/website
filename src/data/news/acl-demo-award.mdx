Kanishk Verma received an Honourable Mention award at ACM IDC 2025 for the work-in-progress paper _From Users to Co-Designers: Youth Participation in Understanding Cyberbullying_. The article highlights the project's focus on co-creating a cyberbullying benchmarking dataset with transition year students through the Young Researcher Assistant Programme.
