The EMNLP 2025 Industry Track paper on BullyBench gives the group a substantial public publication on cyberbullying dataset design, realism evaluation, and benchmark construction. It also publicly records contributions from Kanishk Verma, Sri Balaaji Natarajan Kalaivendan, Joachim Wagner, and Brian Davis.
