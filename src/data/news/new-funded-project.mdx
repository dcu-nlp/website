The 2024 Recognition Awards named Kanishk Verma as Early Stage Researcher of the Year. The citation highlights his Google and Irish Research Council funded PhD research on using AI to detect cyberbullying among preteens and teens and notes his industry engagement with Google's Safety Engineering Centre in Dublin.
